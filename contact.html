// 01 — where it started

Our Origin Story

Every company has a story. Ours starts with a spreadsheet that refused to cooperate.

It was a Thursday night in a cramped campus study room in Columbia, South Carolina. Three students, Stone, Caleb, and Griffin, were supposed to be finishing a group project. Instead, Caleb was telling us about a farmer at the Saturday market who spent every Sunday night guessing how much produce to bring the next week. Too much, and it went to waste. Too little, and customers went home empty-handed.

Griffin opened his laptop and, half as a joke, built a simple model from two years of her sales receipts. Stone grabbed a dry-erase marker and sketched out how she could use it every week without ever touching a line of code. Caleb drove over the next Saturday to show her how it worked. A month later she was wasting less, selling more, and telling every vendor at the market about "those AI kids."

That's when it clicked. Plenty of businesses have heard about artificial intelligence. Almost nobody had shown them how to use it. So we turned that study room into an office, and NewGen AI Solutions was born.

Our mission: help the world understand and use AI to make workflows more efficient, bridging the gap between advanced technology and real-world results through hands-on training and support.

// 05 — you asked, we answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AI going to replace my employees?

Nope. We take the repetitive work off your team's plate, like data entry, sorting emails, and forecasting inventory, so your people can focus on the work that needs a human.

I know nothing about AI. Can you still help?

That's exactly who we built this company for. We explain everything in plain language and train your team so they feel confident long after we leave.

What industries do you work with?

Retail, agriculture, healthcare, insurance, manufacturing, and plenty more. If your business has a problem that involves data, we'd love to hear about it.

How much does it cost?

Every project gets a private quote based on its size, and our prices are built to stay competitive for small businesses. Your first 30-minute consultation is always free.

Is there a robot at the front desk?

Not yet. Right now the front desk is a coffee maker and a very friendly human named Caleb. NOVA covers the late-night shift.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes! Student-run startups get 20% off their first project. We remember what a ramen budget feels like.
